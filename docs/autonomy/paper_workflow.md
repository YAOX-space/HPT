# Paper Workflow

Paper draft structure:

- Introduction: HPT FRT problem, limits of conventional dq/PI, RL motivation.
- Method: HPT model, controller interface, SAC formulation, safety constraints.
- Experiments: scenarios, baselines, metrics, topology1/topology2 validation.
- Discussion: why RL helps, transfer gaps, failure cases, limitations.
- Conclusion: contribution and future work.

Evidence rule: every quantitative claim must link to a committed report,
manifest, or result summary.
